# Liangyi podium prototype

Liangyi Hall receives a scaled copy of the accepted main podium profile over the existing 31 × 14 m foundation envelope, from y=.34 to 1.575. Two eight-step flights remain within the existing z=±9.23 and x=±4 stair envelope, with simple side cheeks and exact top contact.

Only Liangyi's timber and roof assembly moves upward by .8 m. Its body, roof shape, ridge, materials, XZ placement, and the geometry of every other hall remain unchanged. This prototype does not change collision or navigation data.
