# Accepted mature boundary tree scale

Whole-image review accepted the maturity prototype for the 22 outer verge trees. Their deterministic target heights are 9.8–12.08 m; the 24 inner courtyard and garden trees remain on the existing 6.4–7.96 m schedule. Positions, trunk bases, rotations, materials, leaf counts, and LOD thresholds are unchanged.

The woody and foliage instances now consume one shared transform calculation so their authored branch alignment cannot diverge. The source is still a compact young-tree asset enlarged for the boundary composition; crown overlap with nearby roofs is possible and visually natural, while bounds alone do not establish navigable clearance beneath every individual leaf.
